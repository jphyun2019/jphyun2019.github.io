Featured · TIAMAT Scene-Graph Exploration

2025

Online dynamic scene graphs that tell a robot where to explore next

Duke’s entry to DARPA’s TIAMAT challenge, a sim-to-real autonomy competition with a $1M prize pool. A Boston Dynamics Spot builds a 3D scene graph on the fly from streaming RGB-D and uses it to pick exploration frontiers for open-ended natural-language tasks, indoors and outdoors, with no prior map. I built the scene-graph module.

The graph grows online from OWLv2 open-vocabulary detections across five cameras, clustered into 3D boxes and tracked across frames, with spatial relations checked by an LLM. A task parser turns an instruction like “find shelter from the rain” into object queries, and an LLM frontier selector reasons over the partial graph to choose where to go next. Developed in Habitat, MuJoCo, and Isaac Sim, then run on Spot through ROS 2.
